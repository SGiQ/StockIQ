Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 4, 2026

StockiQ is an educational app that teaches investing concepts through short, card-based lessons. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. We designed the app to need as little personal data as possible: you can use every lesson without an account, and nothing about your learning is sold or used for advertising.

The short version. No account is required. If you choose to sign in, we store your email and your learning progress so you can sync and recover it. We do not collect your name or location, show ads, use third-party tracking, or sell your data. You can delete your account and all of its data from within the app at any time.

Who we are

StockiQ (“we”, “us”) is the operator of the StockiQ app and website. For any privacy question or request, contact sgiqbusiness@gmail.com.

Information we collect

  • Email address — only if you choose to sign in. Sign-in is passwordless: we email you a one-time code. We use your email solely to authenticate you and to let you recover and sync your account. We do not send marketing email.
  • A device identifier — a random value created on your device that lets us sync your data and acts as your access key. It is not derived from, or linked to, your real-world identity.
  • Your learning data — lesson progress, saved cards, streaks, and preferences. This lives on your device, and also on our servers if you sign in or sync so it is available across your devices.
  • Limited, non-identifying usage events — for example that a lesson was completed — to understand which features are used and improve the app. These are tied to your account identifier, not to your name, and contain no lesson answers or personal content.
  • Sign in with Apple — if you use it, Apple gives us only an anonymous identifier for this app. We do not receive your name or Apple ID email through it.

What we do not collect

  • No name, phone number, precise location, contacts, or photos.
  • No advertising identifiers, and no third-party advertising or cross-app tracking.
  • We do not sell or rent your personal information to anyone.

The AI tutor (“ASK”)

Some builds let you ask questions about a lesson card. If this uses your own AI provider key, that key is stored only on your device (in the system keychain) and is used to contact the provider directly — StockiQ never receives or stores it. When the tutor is provided through our own service, your question and the on-screen card text are sent to the AI provider to generate an answer, and are not used to train models or for advertising.

Lesson narration

Spoken narration is generated ahead of time and delivered as audio files. Your device does not send your lesson activity or any text to a speech service while you learn.

Where your data is processed

Account and sync data is stored with our infrastructure providers (Railway, which hosts our database, and Cloudflare, which serves content). All data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS). These providers process data on our behalf and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes.

How long we keep it

We keep account data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, we permanently erase your records, devices, sign-in identity, and usage events from our servers, and the app wipes its local data on that device.

Your choices and rights

  • Use without an account. Every lesson works locally with no sign-in and nothing uploaded.
  • Delete your account and data. In the app, open Settings → Account & sync → Delete account. This permanently erases your data from our servers and removes it from the device. You can also email us to request deletion.
  • Access or correct. Contact us and we will help you access or correct the personal data associated with your account.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA, including erasure, which the deletion control above provides.

Children

StockiQ is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Not financial advice

StockiQ is educational only. It does not provide personalized financial, investment, or trading advice, and nothing in the app is a recommendation to buy or sell any security.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the effective date above and, for material changes, provide notice in the app.
