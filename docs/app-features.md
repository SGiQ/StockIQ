## Paper Trading (Practice Portfolio)

- $10,000 simulated starting cash (not real money)
- Trade any ticker at delayed market prices, no fees
- Portfolio summary: total value, return ($ and %), cash remaining
- Value chart: line graph tracking portfolio over time
- Allocation bar: colored position-weight distribution
- Holdings list: shares, avg cost, current value, P/L
- Watchlist integration with quick-trade buttons
- Trade history: chronological buy/sell log
- AI Portfolio Coach: sends composition to AI for educational feedback on sizing and diversification
- Reset to $10,000 anytime

## Gamification

### Streaks
- Current + longest streak tracking
- Monthly calendar of active days
- Streak freezes: protect 1 missed day, max 2, cost 100 gems each
- Milestones at 3, 7, 14, 30, 50, 100, 200, 365 days

### XP
- Earned per lesson completion (first time only)
- Feeds daily goal ring, league ranking, readiness score

### Gems
- Earn: 10/lesson, +5 bonus perfect run, 5/review, 10/daily quest
- Spend: 40 for heart refill, 100 for streak freeze

### Hearts
- 5 max (free tier; Pro/Elite unlimited)
- Lose 1 per wrong quiz answer
- Regen 1 every 4 hours
- Out = can't continue lesson (triggers upsell)
- Review sessions refill 1 heart

### Badges (13+ achievements)
Bronze/silver/gold tiers: First Steps, course completions (9), One Week Strong, Habit Formed, Flawless, Sharp Shooter, Marksman, Strategist, Full Playbook, Checked Out, Curator, Scholar, Full Curriculum.

### Leagues (ready, currently disabled)
7 tiers: Bronze → Diamond. Weekly cohorts of 15. Top 5 promote, bottom 3 relegate. Ranked by weekly XP. Awaiting ~15-20 active users to enable.

### Daily Quests
3 per day from pool (earn XP, finish lessons, ace lessons). 10 gems each.

## Spaced Review

- Resurfaces quiz cards on spacing schedule
- Correct = longer interval, wrong = shorter
- Rewards: 5 gems + 1 heart refill per session
- Feeds Retention component of Market Readiness
- "Keep Sharp" nudge on Learn tab when cards are due

---

## Market Readiness Score

0-100 score, 6 weighted components:
- Coverage 35% — how many lessons completed
- Mastery 25% — quiz accuracy
- Breadth 15% — courses touched
- Retention 10% — review currency
- Assessment 7.5% — readiness quiz score
- Consistency 7.5% — streak strength

5 tiers: Just Starting → Strong Foundation. Degrades without reviews.

### Readiness Assessment
8 cross-course questions, retakeable (best score kept), unlocks badge at 80%+.

## Retention Loops

1. **Daily commitment** — goal set in onboarding, enforced by streak + quests
2. **Streak with freeze economy** — gems buy freezes, making streak precious
3. **Hearts/lives** — wrong answers cost hearts, creating urgency + monetization
4. **Gem economy** — earn through play, spend on freezes + refills
5. **Spaced review** — cards resurface on schedule, feeding Readiness score
6. **Market Readiness** — 6-component score degrades without review
7. **Strategy unlock gates** — finishing lessons unlocks KB strategies
8. **Paper trading + AI coach** — simulated portfolio with real tickers
9. **AI tutor quota** — 5/day free creates value + upgrade pressure
10. **Badges as goal board** — locked badges show what to aim for
